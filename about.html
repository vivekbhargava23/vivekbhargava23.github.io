About

A quant who ships.

I'm a quantitative researcher with roughly six years across econometrics, machine learning, and financial modelling, currently in the Mathematical Finance department at Fraunhofer ITWM. My work spans decision-oriented forecasting and risk and portfolio systems for insurers, banks, and industrial clients — strategic asset allocation, risk-engine engineering, cashflow and liquidity modelling, survival analysis, and high-dimensional time-series forecasting. What I keep coming back to is building end-to-end pipelines that combine statistical rigour with explicit uncertainty, out-of-sample discipline, and clearly defined validity boundaries — and delivering them as production software, not prototypes.
