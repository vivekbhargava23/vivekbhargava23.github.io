Master's thesis

Reservoir computing for mixed-frequency macro forecasting

University of Konstanz · MATLAB, from scratch (~25 modular functions)
Echo State NetworksMIDASVAR
  • Investigated whether Echo State Networks can improve forecasting of quarterly U.S. real GDP growth by exploiting mixed-frequency (monthly + quarterly) data.
  • Designed and implemented three distinct ESN architectures: a stacked-input single reservoir; two coupled reservoirs at separate frequencies with a shared read-out (two state-alignment variants); and a single high-frequency reservoir with output aggregated down.
  • Built the full engine from scratch — leaky-integrator updates, spectral-radius normalisation, sparse reservoir generation, closed-form ridge read-out, burn-in — and validated it on the Mackey–Glass chaotic system first.
  • Benchmarked against U-MIDAS and VAR (both implemented in-framework, with FPE/AIC/HQ/BIC lag selection); hyperparameters tuned by grid search plus Nelder–Mead and constrained optimisation under time-series-aware cross-validation.
Seminar

Cluster-based asset selection for portfolio construction

Unsupervised ML / quantitative finance · MATLAB
ClusteringGMVPBacktesting
  • Applied clustering to the cross-section of S&P 500 returns (205 assets × 443 monthly observations) to reduce the dimensionality of the covariance-estimation problem in portfolio optimisation.
  • Implemented hierarchical clustering (linkage chosen by cophenetic correlation) and K-means (multiple restarts) feeding a common pipeline for a controlled comparison.
  • Built three cluster-based models on the Global Minimum-Variance Portfolio (via pseudoinverse): cluster-mean GMVP, a Certainty-Equivalent-screened version, and a Sharpe-selected representative-asset version.
  • Evaluated with a strict 60-month rolling out-of-sample backtest, explicit proportional transaction costs, and a sweep over cluster count — benchmarked against the naïve 1/N portfolio.
Seminar

Causal analysis of monetary policy shocks

Time-series econometrics / causal inference
SVARExternal instrumentsIRF
  • Identified monetary policy shocks using Structural VARs with external instruments (SVAR B-model).
  • Implemented IV-based identification and analysed the dynamic effects of shocks under explicit identification assumptions.
  • Traced and interpreted impulse responses with attention to interpretability and identification constraints.
