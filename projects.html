B5

Loan-portfolio cashflow forecasting

Insurance client · co-developed · two-module simulation
Survival-weighted EVLeft-truncationHazard modelsAnnuity dynamicsPaper in prep.
  • Co-developed a two-module loan-portfolio cashflow engine — disbursements and repayments modelled separately, every parameter calibrated from historical contract data and backtested against realised cashflows per month and booking entity, across both the existing book and forecasted new business.
  • Payout side: built an empirical, volume-weighted payout curve indexed by months-since-commitment with an outstanding-obligation (negative-cumsum) profile; each contract's remaining obligation is spread along the curve tail from its current age, segmented by loan purpose (construction vs. purchase).
  • Repayment entry under left-truncation: estimated time-to-full-payout with a duration estimator conditioned on survival to the contract's current age (the valid comparison set is contracts still un-paid-out at that age), plus a capital-weighted payout-timing distribution for contracts with no history yet.
  • Repayment streams: combined a standard annuity (interest on the outstanding balance, instalment fixed on the approved amount), a deterministic seasonal model for partial prepayments, and an age-dependent hazard for full early repayment that peaks at the statutory ~10-year fixed-rate exit right observed directly in the data.
  • Kept the simulation tractable by replacing per-contract Monte-Carlo branching with a single expected-value trajectory weighted by a running survival probability — full-repayment cash P_alive · pₙ · Sₜ with survival decayed each month — and modelled prolongation (nominal-rate reset from swap rates at fixed-rate end) and expiry. Joint paper in preparation.

B7

Warranty-claim anomaly detection

High-performance automotive OEM · supervised + unsupervised ensemble
Anomaly detectionEnsemble stackingIsolation ForestWeak / partial labelsDistribution shiftReproducible pipeline
  • Architected a five-model anomaly-detection ensemble fusing supervised learners (random forest, feed-forward neural net, gradient-boosted trees) with unsupervised detectors (group-conditional Z-score, Isolation Forest), reconciled by an L2 linear-stacking meta-learner fitted on out-of-sample base scores.
  • Resolved a temporal label-decay / covariate-shift regime by confining supervised estimation to the densely-audited window while delegating out-of-window detection to the unsupervised models, whose reference statistics — cohort moments and isolation path-length — re-estimate without labels.
  • Engineered a weak-supervision labelling layer fusing audit recoveries, booking-correction pairs, and reviewed lists; anchored the empirical class prior to the audited source and propagated it through ratio equalisation and a fit-time representativeness filter held out of population scoring.
  • Productionised as a file-mediated train/score decoupling — fit-once, score-forever — over a relational claim schema aggregated to claim grain, with type-safe composite-key joins, regression-tested transforms, and a distributional guardrail (score-range, mean, and volume invariants) gating the HANA write.
