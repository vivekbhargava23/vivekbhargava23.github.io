Statistical estimation & inference

M.Sc. training · basis for client model choices

Regression, MLE and QMLE, GMM, IV/2SLS and M-estimation; asymptotic and bootstrap inference, Wald/LR/LM testing, and Monte Carlo checks of finite-sample behaviour.

Where I used it: Implemented from scratch during my M.Sc. in econometrics; it is the basis for how I choose and validate models in the client work.
Methods in detail

Estimation

OLS and GLS; maximum likelihood with scores, Hessians and information matrices; quasi-ML under misspecification; GMM with efficient two-step weighting; M- and minimum-distance estimation.

Checked by: heteroskedasticity- and autocorrelation-robust covariances; Wald, likelihood-ratio and Lagrange-multiplier tests.

Identification

Endogeneity from omitted variables, simultaneity and measurement error; IV/2SLS and control functions; weak- and many-instrument problems.

Checked by: overidentification and Hausman–Wu specification tests.

Uncertainty

Parametric, nonparametric and dependent-data bootstrap; Monte Carlo experiments on bias, variance, test size and power.

Checked by: Jarque–Bera, Kolmogorov–Smirnov and QQ diagnostics of distributional assumptions.

Numerical optimisation & learning

Production (team system) · M.Sc. thesis

Loss functions and gradient-based estimation, Lasso and Elastic Net, cross-validated model selection, and constrained and multi-objective (Pareto) optimisation.

Where I used it: Weighted-Tchebycheff Pareto allocation in an insurer's asset-allocation system (team system; I own its decision-support layer); constrained hyperparameter search in my M.Sc. thesis.
Methods in detail

Objectives and solvers

L1, L2, Huber, hinge and negative log-likelihood losses; analytical gradients and Hessians; batch and stochastic gradient descent, AdaGrad and Adam.

Checked by: convergence diagnostics and comparison against closed-form or benchmark estimates.

Regularisation

Ridge, Lasso, Elastic Net and adaptive Lasso; regularisation paths; pseudoinverse methods for ill-conditioned, high-dimensional problems.

Checked by: leave-one-out, K-fold and nested cross-validation, with a final check on untouched test data.

Constrained and multi-objective

Bounds and nonlinear risk constraints; non-dominated solutions, extreme portfolios, weighted-Tchebycheff scalarisation and objective-space subdivision.

Checked by: feasibility constraints and sensitivity analysis of the trade-offs.

Where I used it: Budget forecasting over ~3,000 cost series (sole developer); mixed-frequency alignment in the bank risk engine; SVAR with external instruments in a research seminar.
Methods in detail

Univariate

Stationarity, ACF/PACF identification and Box–Jenkins ARIMA; multi-step forecast recursions and prediction intervals.

Multivariate

VAR with information-criterion lag selection and Granger causality; Johansen cointegration and VECM; SVAR with Cholesky or external-instrument identification, impulse responses and variance decompositions; Bayesian estimation.

Master's thesis

Reservoir computing for mixed-frequency macro forecasting

University of Konstanz · MATLAB, from scratch (~25 modular functions)
Echo State NetworksMIDASVAR
  • Investigated whether Echo State Networks can improve forecasting of quarterly U.S. real GDP growth by exploiting mixed-frequency (monthly + quarterly) data.
  • Designed and implemented three distinct ESN architectures: a stacked-input single reservoir; two coupled reservoirs at separate frequencies with a shared read-out (two state-alignment variants); and a single high-frequency reservoir with output aggregated down.
  • Built the full engine from scratch — leaky-integrator updates, spectral-radius normalisation, sparse reservoir generation, closed-form ridge read-out, burn-in — and validated it on the Mackey–Glass chaotic system first.
  • Benchmarked against U-MIDAS and VAR (both implemented in-framework, with FPE/AIC/HQ/BIC lag selection); hyperparameters tuned by grid search plus Nelder–Mead and constrained optimisation under time-series-aware cross-validation.
Seminar

Cluster-based asset selection for portfolio construction

Unsupervised ML / quantitative finance · MATLAB
ClusteringGMVPBacktesting
  • Applied clustering to the cross-section of S&P 500 returns (205 assets × 443 monthly observations) to reduce the dimensionality of the covariance-estimation problem in portfolio optimisation.
  • Implemented hierarchical clustering (linkage chosen by cophenetic correlation) and K-means (multiple restarts) feeding a common pipeline for a controlled comparison.
  • Built three cluster-based models on the Global Minimum-Variance Portfolio (via pseudoinverse): cluster-mean GMVP, a Certainty-Equivalent-screened version, and a Sharpe-selected representative-asset version.
  • Evaluated with a strict 60-month rolling out-of-sample backtest, explicit proportional transaction costs, and a sweep over cluster count — benchmarked against the naïve 1/N portfolio.
